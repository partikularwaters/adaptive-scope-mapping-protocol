## 1. Purpose and Current Reality
- Current situation: Maple Street has held informal autumn gatherings for years but nothing coordinated since 2019.
- Problem or opportunity: Residents want a single afternoon event celebrating local food growers and makers without a commercial vendor fair feel.
- Desired change: A one-day neighborhood harvest festival on the closed block with local food, music, and kids' activities.
- Why now: New community garden opened; momentum and volunteer interest are high.
- Consequence of no action: Event idea dissipates; garden opening goes uncelebrated.

## 2. Primary Person and Owner
- Primary beneficiary: Maple Street residents and nearby families who want a free, local gathering.
- Accountable decision-maker: Elena Vasquez
- Other materially relevant roles: City parks liaison (permits); volunteer safety lead (TBD by 2026-09-12)

## 3. Scope
### In scope
- One afternoon event (4 hours) on a single closed block
- Up to 15 local grower/maker tables (no fee to participate)
- Two acoustic music sets
- Kids' activity corner (crafts + pumpkin decorating)
- Basic signage, waste stations, and volunteer shift plan
- City street-closure and park-use permit applications

### Conditional
- Road closure — included only if city approves by 2026-10-01

### Out of scope
- Ticket sales or paid admission
- Alcohol service
- Professional security firm (volunteer marshals only)
- Multi-day or recurring series (possible follow-up if successful)

### External dependencies
- City street-closure permit — city liaison — application due 2026-09-20
- Portable restroom rental — vendor quote needed — Open
